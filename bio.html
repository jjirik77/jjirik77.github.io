About

Bridging academic programs and industry needs

Jiri Jirik is a seasoned cybersecurity professional with more than 25 years of experience spanning industry and academia. As Director of the Education Pathway National Center (EPNC) at Moraine Valley Community College, he leads the design and implementation of comprehensive cybersecurity and artificial intelligence curricula for K–12 students, higher education and adult learners.

In this role, he engages stakeholders across academia, industry and the public sector in the collaborative design of curricula that serve learners across the educational spectrum. He works on cybersecurity initiatives funded by the National Science Foundation (NSF), the National Security Agency (NSA) and other public and private organizations.

His private-sector career in IT built a strong practice of working with people at every level of an organization to create and deliver technical solutions for internal and external stakeholders.
